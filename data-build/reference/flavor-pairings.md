# Flavor & pairing reference (researched 2026-06)

## Protein seasonings
- Chicken: garlic powder, paprika (smoked or sweet), onion powder, lemon/lemon zest, thyme, oregano, rosemary, cumin; sweet-savory base that takes any cuisine. Cajun or jerk for heat, lemon-herb for clean.
- Beef: black pepper + cumin (the classic robust pair), garlic, smoked paprika, chili powder, coriander, onion powder; rosemary for steak, dry-mustard + onion-powder steak rub. Beef wants STRONG seasoning.
- Pork: rosemary, thyme, sage, caraway, paprika, fennel seed, mustard, garlic; sweet-heat works (chili + a touch of sweet). Apple/maple notes pair well.
- Salmon: coriander (warm, citrusy, nutty), dill, lemon, garlic, black pepper; treat it bold like beef - soy/garlic/blackened or red pepper flakes also work because salmon is fatty enough to stand up.
- White fish (cod, tilapia, halibut): keep it LIGHT - garlic, parsley, dill, lemon, Old Bay, paprika, a little butter. Delicate flesh, don't overpower. Cajun OK if you want heat.
- Shrimp: garlic, lemon/lemon zest, paprika, Cajun/Old Bay, red pepper flakes, parsley, chili; super versatile - goes light-herb OR heavy-garlic-and-spice. Cooks fast so use fast-hitting seasonings.
- Tofu: needs assertive seasoning - garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, cumin, turmeric (color + gentle flavor), chili powder, nutritional yeast, soy/coconut aminos; press it dry first so seasoning sticks.
- Eggs: salt, black pepper, chives, dill, paprika, basil, tarragon, chili flakes, everything-bagel seasoning; eggs take almost any herb - dill, chervil, parsley, tarragon all classic.
- Turkey: poultry seasoning (sage, marjoram, thyme, rosemary, celery seed), basil, tarragon, lemon zest, garlic, onion powder, paprika; lean so season generously and add fat/moisture.
- Lamb: rosemary, thyme, mint, cumin, coriander, garlic, oregano; Mediterranean/Middle-Eastern lean - garam masala, za'atar, harissa all sing with lamb's gaminess.

## Cuisine sauces
- Indian: raita or plain Greek yogurt (cooling, high-protein), mango chutney as a sweet finish, tikka/curry spice rubs; lean on yogurt-based sauces over cream-based for low cal.
- Thai: light peanut sauce (PB2-based ~50 cal/2 tbsp), sweet chili (~10 cal/tbsp), nuoc cham / nam pla finishing sauce (~14 cal/2 tbsp), fresh lime + cilantro; fish sauce + lime + chili is the flavor backbone, nearly calorie-free.
- Mexican: salsa verde, pico de gallo, chipotle-in-adobo (~5-15 cal/tbsp, huge smoky flavor for the calories), cilantro-lime, light enchilada sauce (~40 cal/100g); these are some of the best flavor-per-calorie sauces that exist.
- Greek/Med: tzatziki (Greek-yogurt + cucumber + garlic + dill), lemon-oregano (ladolemono), tahini-lemon (richer, use sparingly), za'atar; Greek-yogurt base is the low-cal MVP here.
- Italian: marinara/pomodoro, fresh basil + garlic, balsamic, a light sprinkle of parmesan as a flavor-bomb finish; skip cream and oil-heavy pestos, lean tomato + herb.
- Korean: gochujang (~56 cal/tbsp, intense), ssamjang (~50 cal/tbsp), sugar-free Korean BBQ marinades; gochujang thinned with rice vinegar + a little sweetener makes a huge-flavor, low-cal glaze.
- Japanese: ponzu (~10 cal/tbsp - citrus-soy, incredible flavor-per-calorie), tamari/soy, miso glaze, sugar-free teriyaki; ponzu is the secret weapon - bright, savory, almost no calories.
- American/BBQ: sugar-free BBQ (G Hughes ~5-10 cal/tbsp, Noble Made ~20 cal/2tbsp), mustard-based Carolina sauces, Buffalo/Frank's (~0 cal); sugar-free BBQ brands make this whole category nearly free.

## More low-cal sauces
- Chimichurri: ~120-200 cal per 100g if you cut the oil in half (full-fat store versions run ~400 cal/100g, ~80-90 cal/tbsp - that's the catch) (homemade or Trader Joe's / Whole Foods)
- Gochujang: ~220 cal/100g, ~50-56 cal/tbsp (Chung Jung One / Haechandle / Mother-in-Law's)
- Ssamjang: ~200 cal/100g, ~50 cal/tbsp (Chung Jung One / Wang)
- Harissa paste: ~100-150 cal/100g (basic), ~25-30 cal/tbsp (Mina / NY Shuk / Trader Joe's)
- Chipotle in adobo: ~97 cal/100g, ~5-15 cal/tbsp (La Costena / Embasa / San Marcos)
- Light peanut sauce (PB2-based): ~150-180 cal/100g, ~50 cal per 2 tbsp (homemade with PB2 powder + soy + lime + sweetener)
- Mango chutney: ~140-240 cal/100g depending on brand, ~50 cal/tbsp (Sharwood's / Patak's (or look for 'reduced sugar'))
- Romesco: ~115-207 cal/100g (mostly from nuts/oil) (homemade or Trader Joe's)
- Tahini-lemon sauce: ~250-300 cal/100g thinned (straight tahini-heavy versions hit ~500/100g) (homemade or Soom / Seed + Mill tahini)
- Ponzu: ~50-65 cal/100g, ~10 cal/tbsp (Kikkoman / Mizkan)
- Nuoc cham: ~45-50 cal/100g, ~14 cal per 2 tbsp (homemade (fish sauce + lime + chili + sweetener) or Red Boat)
- Zhug / skhug: ~150-250 cal/100g (cut oil to lower it), use by the spoon (homemade or Trader Joe's)
- Enchilada sauce (red or green): ~40 cal/100g, ~15-25 cal per 1/4 cup (Las Palmas / Hatch / Siete (light versions))
- Sugar-free BBQ: G Hughes ~5-10 cal/tbsp; Noble Made ~20 cal/2 tbsp; Primal ~3g carbs (G Hughes / Noble Made (The New Primal) / Primal Kitchen)
- Greek-yogurt buffalo / hot-sauce blends: ~50-60 cal/100g (Frank's RedHot + nonfat Greek yogurt (homemade))
